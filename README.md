# Lichenkang

Open-source developer building AI workspaces and developer tools.

Creator and maintainer of [Lithe IDEA](https://github.com/1lck/Lithe-IDEA). Core developer of [Cherry Studio](https://github.com/CherryHQ/cherry-studio).

I build tools that bring AI into everyday development workflows, from cross-platform IDEs and AI workspaces to extensible tooling. My focus is on thoughtful architecture, reliable execution, and an experience that makes complex capabilities feel straightforward.

### Projects

- **[Lithe IDEA](https://github.com/1lck/Lithe-IDEA)** — A lightweight, cross-platform IDE for the AI era, with tools and services available on demand.
- **[Cherry Studio](https://github.com/CherryHQ/cherry-studio)** — Core development of an AI workspace for models, agents, and productivity.
- **[Skills Dock](https://github.com/1lck/Skills-Dock)** — An app for discovering, organizing, and managing AI skills across tools.
- **[AI Resume Form Filling Assistant](https://github.com/1lck/AI-Resume-Form-Filling-Assistant)** — A browser extension that turns resume content into AI-assisted form filling.

I also contribute to [Nacos](https://github.com/alibaba/nacos/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3A1lck+is%3Amerged) and [JetBrains CC GUI](https://github.com/zhukunpenglinyutong/jetbrains-cc-gui/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3A1lck+is%3Amerged).
